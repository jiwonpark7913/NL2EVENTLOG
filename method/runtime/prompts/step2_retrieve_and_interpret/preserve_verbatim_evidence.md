
Evidence quoting contract: text in quotation marks that you attribute to evidence or to the request is checked by machine as an exact substring: same case, punctuation and whitespace. A mismatch rejects your answer.
- Prefer paraphrase without quotation marks. Quote only when exactness matters.
- When you quote, copy one contiguous stretch. No ellipses, no joining of pieces, no corrected typos, no translation. Quote only text you can actually see; evidence may end mid-sentence, so do not complete it.
- Never put a changed phrase, label or paraphrase inside quotation marks.
- When you cite evidence, give its evidence ID exactly as supplied.
