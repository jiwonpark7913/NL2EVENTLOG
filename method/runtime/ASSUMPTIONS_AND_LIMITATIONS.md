# Assumptions and limitations

## Domain and extraction evidence

- The source database and Original DD make relevant identity, business occurrence, time and attribution facts inspectable. Sparse or obsolete DD, hidden lifecycles, code ambiguity, undocumented relationships and unstable event keys can block construction or yield incomplete evidence. Declared FK reachability must be distinguished from a meaningful role.
- Stage 1 uses bounded profiling and LLM-assisted reusable proposals. Both inference models share the same three Haiku-built EEGs and Indexes. The comparison supplies no evidence for Gemini graph construction, operational database drift, production privacy settings or arbitrarily large graphs.
- Qualified full delivery is full relative to the constructed EEG and its execution eligibility. A valid Case may expose no ED. The detailed Event view exposes eligible connected concepts and roles after Case Selection. Graph connectivity does not establish that a Case-conditioned population contains any occurrence.
- A supported natural-language scope is an existing structural Case population restriction. General calendar/threshold/attribute predicates, arbitrary analytical expressions, attribute enrichment and downstream process analysis are outside the research contract. An unfulfilled material constraint should be disclosed. No generic predicate generator is present.

## Interpretation and execution

- Case and scope are committed before detailed Event Selection. Early incorrect Case choices can change all subsequent exposure. S0 supplies model-generated intent text to both selectors. S3 removes both its generation and use, and records activity mode at Event Selection. Their paired comparison measures that combined intervention. The earlier S2 contrast combines the joint role with early Activity capability cards. Neither contrast identifies the independent effect of its components.
- A request can have multiple activities and attribution roles across source tables, while the expected log uses one Case ET and at most one Case assignment per physical occurrence. Documented assignments to multiple in-scope Cases are given an explicit unresolved reason. They require a separate output and scoring contract.
- A model can omit an available activity, select the wrong temporal phase or use the wrong adjudicable role while satisfying a structural schema. DD and path review are evidence rather than a final semantic gate. The normalized output checks constrain physical integrity; they cannot establish that the model chose the intended business occurrence.
- Exact duplicate path assignments are collapsed. Distinct physical occurrences with the same timestamp and label are retained. Additional trace-level frequency or order diagnostics remain observations rather than automatic repair signals. Source witnesses and validation depend on a frozen snapshot and known physical assumptions.
- Complete menus, large ED closures and path enumeration have context and scaling costs. In the historical S2 comparison, the capability projection was snapshot-cached in the process. Context-budget and provider output bounds can still yield explicit failures.

## Measurement and external validity

- The 72 fresh conditions (36 pairs) comprise two models, two arms, 12 paired AirlineFlow requests and two small synthetic sets of three fixture requests each. Paired arms share requests and graph evidence. Individual log events are dependent observations. The panels establish limited development behavior and support no final generalization claims.
- Prior fixed-answer replay checks deterministic preservation, while the new native run measures separately generated responses. Consecutive archivals by different vintages or prompts cannot stand in for a contemporaneous statistical replication. Native samples are one observed response sequence per arm/request/model.
- Original v4 and v5-dev preserve the same physical and completion measurements; v5-dev adds fixed development word-equivalence decisions. Exact internal Case/ED/role outcomes and final logs can disagree for physically or semantically equivalent alternatives. V5-dev is still a development policy. The second independent Activity Concept Equivalence human adjudication remains incomplete.
- Gemini price is derived from real metered use at the frozen conservative paid-list policy. Invoice, free-tier and credits remain unverified. Cost, latency and stochastic outputs are specific to the recorded settings. The full corpus, Direct benchmark and final evaluation remain pending. Single observed response sequences and model-setting changes can affect both correctness and resource use. No significance or cross-domain generalization follows from the observed rankings.
- Evaluator isolation uses allowed public inputs and scoped offline scoring with verified bytes. This reduces accidental leakage. Pretraining exposure, developer adaptation, and bias in authored definitions and labels remain unverified.
